The mental load

If everyone comes to you first, this one's for you.

You're the default parent. The school has your number. You know where the passports are, when the insurance renews, which child needs the trip form signed by Friday. None of it is written down — it just lives in your head, running quietly in the background, all day, every day.

That's the mental load. It's invisible, it's exhausting, and it doesn't clock off. And the moment it slips — an expired passport, a forgotten consent form — it stops being invisible very fast.

You don't need another app to fill in. You need somewhere to put it down.

Privacy

Your family's data stays your family's.

The assistant only ever sees your own family's documents and dates — nothing else, and no other family's data. It answers from what you've added, not from the open internet, so it doesn't make things up. Every family's vault is isolated and private by design.

No selling your data. No training a model on your kids' school forms. Just a private place that helps you remember.

FAQ

The questions parents ask first.

Is my family's data private?
Yes — that's foundational, not a feature. Your documents and dates are isolated to your family and never shared with anyone else. The assistant only ever sees your own data, and we don't sell it or use your family's life to train models.
Does the AI make things up?
No. It answers only from your family's own documents and dates. If the answer isn't in your data, it tells you it doesn't know — it won't invent one.
Is it on iPhone and Android?
Yes — Family AI Assistant is built mobile-first for both iOS and Android. Early-access spots will roll out to both.
What does it cost?
Right now, early access is free for our founding families. We'll be upfront about pricing well before anything changes — and early families will get looked after.
Do I have to enter everything manually like other apps?
No. You add your documents once, and the app does the work — pulling out expiry dates, renewals and school tasks for you. It's designed to reduce what you have to track, not add to it.
